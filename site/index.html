Steps privacy policy

Last updated 30/09/2026

Steps (until September 2026, Amluto Steps) is a Windows app, and an extension for Chrome and Edge, that records a task as you do it, click by click, and turns it into a step-by-step guide.

Everything it records stays on your PC, or in the folders you choose. The app never sends it to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we never see your guides. The only thing it ever asks online is whether there's a new version.

Who we are

Steps is made by Amluto Solutions Ltd, a UK company. If you have a question about this policy, email steps@amluto.com.

Steps records nothing until you press Record, and it shows a recording bar the whole time it is recording.

What it never does

  • It never reads your keyboard unless you tick "Record what's typed" for that recording. Then it reads it only while that recording runs, not while it's paused, and the recording bar says "Keys recorded" the whole time.
  • Even then, it never keeps what you type in a password box, in a field whose name or label says it holds a PIN, a one-time code, a card or account number, a sort code or memorable information, in apps that are never recorded, or in Steps itself. A password a terminal hides as you type it is never seen.
  • In commands, anything after a name such as -Password or -Token is replaced with •••• before it's saved, and blurred in the step's screenshot.
  • Card numbers and bank account numbers (IBAN) you type are cut to their last four characters before they're saved; so are National Insurance numbers, email addresses and phone numbers typed into fields and documents.
  • It never records apps that you, or your organisation, have excluded. Anything of theirs that shows in a screenshot is blacked out.
  • It never sends what it records anywhere, and it never uploads your guides.

Where your guides are kept

Guides are saved as ordinary files on your PC, or in library folders you choose. If you choose a folder that syncs, such as OneDrive or SharePoint, the files go wherever that service puts them, under your or your organisation's arrangements with it, not ours. A guide only leaves your PC when you export it, share it or put it in a synced folder yourself.

Before any export, the app shows every screenshot as it will appear and points out possible personal details that aren't blurred. It can miss things, so check each screenshot.

What stays in the app's own folders on your PC

  • A recording in progress, until you save it as a guide or discard it.
  • Text read from screenshots, used only to suggest what to blur. It never goes into guides or exports, and is removed after 30 days unused. (Text read to find a secret to blur in a command's screenshot isn't kept at all.)
  • Logs of what the app did, kept for 30 days. They never include what you type, screenshots or text read from them.

If you ask us for help

The app can make a support file with its logs, versions and settings, with your library names, folders and your name taken out. It is made only when you ask, you can look inside it first, and it is sent only if you attach it to an email yourself. We use what you send only to help with your problem, and don't keep it for longer than that needs.

Updates

The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store. The .msi version your IT team installs never looks for updates.

The version installed from our own download, and the portable version once you switch on Update automatically, ask steps.amluto.com whether there's a new version: shortly after it opens, at most once a day, and when you press Check for updates. The request says nothing about you, your PC or your guides, not even which version you have. Like any request to a website, it shows our web host your IP address (see This website below).

A new version is downloaded in the background and installed the next time the app opens (or straight away, if you choose Restart now), and only if it carries Amluto's signature. Switch off Update automatically in Settings > About (the portable version starts with it off) and the app never asks unless you press Check for updates; your IT team can turn the check off altogether.

Microsoft's parts of the picture

Steps runs on Windows and draws its screens with Microsoft Edge WebView2. The Microsoft Store, Windows and WebView2 are Microsoft's, and what they collect is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement, not this policy.

Steps for Chrome, Edge and Firefox

The browser extension works like the app, inside the browser, with these differences.

  • It records only while you record from its side panel, which shows the recording and each step as it's recorded. Only then does it add its recording script to the pages you open, and it takes it out when you stop.
  • For each click it keeps the name of what you clicked, a screenshot of the part of the page you can see, the page's title, and the site's address without its path. When you move to another site, that's a step too.
  • It reads what you type into a field, or into a text editor on a page (such as an email you're writing), only in a recording where you tick "Record what's typed", and never from a password box or a field whose name or label says it holds a secret. In an editor it keeps only what you typed, not what was already there. This includes fields and editors inside frames on a page.
  • If you let it use the fonts installed on your computer (Chrome asks you once), it looks through them for the fonts your brands name, and puts those into PDFs. Nothing about your fonts leaves your computer.
  • To suggest what to blur, it reads the words shown on the page as each screenshot is taken, but never what's in a field or an editor. They're kept in the browser, never go into guides or exports, and are removed after 30 days unused.
  • It never records sites that you, or your organisation, have excluded: no clicks, typing or screenshots.
  • Your guides and settings are kept in the browser's storage for the extension on your computer. They aren't synced to your Google or Microsoft account and are never sent to us. Removing the extension deletes them, so export any guide you want to keep first. In Chrome and Edge you can also keep guides in a folder you choose, such as a OneDrive or SharePoint folder your team shares, as the app does. Steps reads and writes only that folder, and only once you've allowed it; whoever the folder is shared with can see what's in it.
  • It asks nothing online. The Chrome Web Store, Edge Add-ons or Firefox Add-ons updates it, and the browsers and their stores are covered by Google's, Microsoft's and Mozilla's own privacy policies.

If your organisation provides the app

Your IT team can set some choices for everyone, such as turning off "Record what's typed", adding apps or sites that are never recorded, or adding words that are always blurred. Guides kept in your organisation's shared folders are managed under its own policies.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts, and loads nothing from other sites. It and the update folder at steps.amluto.com are hosted by SiteGround, which, like any web host, keeps short-lived access logs, such as IP addresses, to keep them running and secure.

Your rights

The app sends us nothing about you, so the only personal information we hold is what you choose to send us, such as an email asking for help. You can ask us for a copy of it, or to correct or delete it, at steps@amluto.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Steps is a work tool and isn't aimed at children.
