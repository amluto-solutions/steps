## Microsoft Store

### Description

(Up to 10,000 characters.)

```
Steps turns a task you do once into a step-by-step guide.

Press New recording and do the task the way you always do. Steps writes each click down as a step with a screenshot and plain wording, like Click "Save". Tidy it up, blur anything private, add your brand, and share it as a PDF, a Word document or an interactive web page.

Made for IT teams, support desks, trainers and anyone who writes how-to guides.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your guides stay on your PC or in the folder you choose, such as OneDrive or SharePoint. No account, no analytics, and nothing is sent to us.
• Emails, phone numbers, postcodes, card numbers, and names or IDs beside their labels are found in every screenshot, on your PC. Blur them one at a time or all at once.
• What you type is only recorded when you tick "Record what's typed" for that recording, and password fields never are.

GUIDES THAT LOOK FINISHED
• Reword steps, add notes and coloured tip and warning boxes, draw arrows and boxes, and crop.
• Choose how steps read: casual, plain language or formal.
• Your logo, colours and fonts on every export, with a brand for each client.
• Commands typed in PowerShell or Windows Terminal become code blocks with their own Copy button, with passwords and tokens masked.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• Steps speaks 38 languages. It follows the language Windows uses, or the one you choose in Settings.
• A guide can hold its words in several languages: recorded steps are worded in each of them for you, and you write your own notes in each.
• Export a PDF or Word document in any of them, or a web page that opens in each reader's own language.

SHARE IT YOUR WAY
• PDF, one step per page or several.
• Word, with real headings and alt text on every screenshot.
• Copy and paste into Outlook, Teams, SharePoint or Confluence.
• An interactive web page that plays the guide step by step, works offline and prints as a plain list.

READY FOR IT
• Group Policy and Intune settings for shared libraries, company brands, the app's language, apps that are never recorded, and switching off typing capture. The IT guide is at steps.amluto.com/it.

Free, with no account.
```

## Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons

### Summary

(Up to 132 characters.)

```
Record a task in Chrome click by click and turn it into a step-by-step guide with screenshots. Nothing leaves your computer.
```

### Description

(Up to 16,000 characters.)

```
Steps turns a task you do in the browser into a step-by-step guide.

Open Steps from the toolbar, press Start recording and do the task as normal. Each click becomes a step with a screenshot and words such as Click "Save", written for you. When you stop, review the guide: edit the words, blur anything private, add arrows, boxes and notes, and reorder or delete steps. Then export it as a PDF, a Word document or an interactive web page that plays step by step, or copy it into an email, Teams or SharePoint.

Private by design
• It records only while you're recording, and the side panel shows each step as it's recorded.
• Everything stays on your computer: in your browser, or in a folder you choose. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sent to us.
• What you type is recorded only if you tick "Record what's typed" for that recording, and never from password boxes or fields that hold secrets.
• Sites you choose are never recorded.
• Before any export it shows every screenshot and points out possible personal details that aren't blurred.

In your language
Steps speaks 38 languages and follows your browser's language, or the one you choose in Settings. A guide can hold its words in several languages, and a web page export opens in each reader's own.

For organisations
IT can install it for everyone and set choices through Chrome or Edge policy: sites that are never recorded, words that are always blurred, extra field names that are never read, the language, and turning off "Record what's typed".

Guides can be shared with people using the Steps app for Windows as .amlsteps files, or through a OneDrive or SharePoint folder you both use as a library (in Chrome and Edge).
```
